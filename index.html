From the Principal · Del Director

English

Dear High School Team, last week was long and demanding, and you met it with remarkable commitment. Alongside our regular classes, we hosted the AASCA Knowledge Bowl, welcoming eight schools from across Central America to our campus for four days.

It was a great success. Congratulations to our Varsity team, coached by Ms. Ana Lucía García, who went undefeated in the round robin and finished fourth overall, and to our Junior Varsity team, coached by Ms. Karol Juárez, who closed the tournament with a win to take fifth place. You represented DelCampo with talent, poise and sportsmanship.

Behind every match, meal, schedule and smile, there was someone from our community making it happen. To every teacher, committee member, staff member, usher and volunteer, whether your role was visible or behind the scenes: thank you. An event like this happens only when everyone commits, and you did.

Don't just take it from me. Below is what our guests had to say. This is what High Standards looks like in action. Thank you for showing it.

With gratitude,

📊 Progress Reports · Reportes de Avance

Every class, every student, with grades · Cada clase, cada estudiante, con calificaciones

✍️
Mon 23 · end of dayAll grades in PowerSchoolTodas las notas en PowerSchool
⚙️
Tue 24Progress reports generatedSe generan los reportes
📧
Wed 25 · first thingSent to families electronicallySe envían a las familias de forma electrónica

Because this is a short week, progress reports will be generated on Tuesday and sent to families electronically first thing Wednesday morning. Every class must have grades entered in PowerSchool by the end of the day on Monday, November 23.

A progress report that reaches a family with a class showing no grades tells a family we have nothing to report about their child's learning. That's not the message we want to send. Timely, accurate grades are part of our professional responsibilities.

Como es una semana corta, los reportes de avance se generarán el martes y se enviarán a las familias de forma electrónica a primera hora del miércoles. Todas las clases deben tener calificaciones registradas en PowerSchool a más tardar al final del día del lunes 23 de noviembre.

Un reporte de avance que llega a una familia con una clase sin calificaciones le dice a la familia que no tenemos nada que informar sobre el aprendizaje de su hijo. Ese no es el mensaje que queremos enviar. Registrar calificaciones a tiempo y con precisión es parte de nuestras responsabilidades profesionales.

⚠️ No progress report will be sent with a class without grades.
Ningún reporte de avance se enviará con una clase sin calificaciones.
